# Forge OS — Brand Guidelines

## Logo

The Forge logo depicts an anvil and hammer — symbols of creation, transformation, and craftsmanship.
Sparks radiate from the impact point, representing AI intelligence igniting from the forge.

### Variants
| File | Usage |
|------|-------|
| `assets/logo.svg` | Primary — wordmark + icon, horizontal |
| `assets/icon.svg` | Icon only — app icon, favicon, taskbar |

## Typography

- **Headings/Logo**: Inter (sans-serif, geometric)
- **Monospace/Code**: JetBrains Mono
- **UI Body**: Inter / system font stack

## Tone

- Bold, confident, warm
- Craftsmanship over complexity
- "We forge the future, you shape it"
